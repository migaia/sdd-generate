# Cross-SDD interfaces

`exports` and `consumes` are needed only for an interface crossing child boundaries. An export points to its produced Asset; a consumer names `{document, export, version}` using the producing child ID, export ID and exact version. The consumer's Bundle requires the producing Asset, which makes the dependency concrete. Versions here are design targets, never evidence that the artifact was built. The producer is the single source of the behaviour it exports: an export may list `semantics` (the producer's requirement or acceptance IDs a consumer may rely on) and then records their `fingerprint`, which `validate` computes from those clauses' prose and reports as stale (`SDD_V2_EXPORT_FINGERPRINT_STALE`) when a listed clause changes without a new fingerprint and version. Prose names an export without its version and points to `exports`; a restated version that differs is `SDD_V2_EXPORT_VERSION_PROSE_STALE` unless the phrase is explicitly historical. A consumer of such an export pins the same `fingerprint` in `consumes` (a differing pin is `consumer-pin-stale`), and cites the clauses an acceptance exercises in `relies_on` (`{"A6": [{"document", "export", "clause"}]}`) instead of restating them: its prose asserts only its own observable effects of each producer outcome. A consumer acceptance that names the producer's exported symbols without `relies_on` is a `SDD_V2_PRODUCER_SEMANTICS_RESTATED` candidate. When a consumer hands one of its own surfaces to a consumed export, the clause that does so is listed in `delegations` (`{"S7": {"document", "export", "delta": {"R4": "BC4"}, "errors": {"INVALID_OPTION": "wrap:PIPELINE_MODE_MISMATCH"}}}`): one delta per rule in the export's `semantics` (`identical`, `BC<n>` or `guarded:<clause>`) and one disposition per code in the export's `errors` (`propagate`, `wrap:<CODE>` or `unreachable`); a missing row is `SDD_V2_DELEGATION_INCOMPLETE`. A consumer that names its `error_registry` gets `SDD_V2_ERROR_CODE_UNREACHABLE` candidates for registered codes nothing under its writes throws and no BC removes; a step calling an export's symbol without a delegation, and a "same as R<n>" claim whose exceptions miss a non-identical delta, are candidates too.
