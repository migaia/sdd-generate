# Inventories

`inventories` (optional) lists, per guarded or migrated requirement, the entry points that can break it: `[{"id":"I1","requirement":"R1","kind":"invariant|surface|preserved-branch|cost-path|current-behaviour","statement":"…","entry_points":[{"name":"unUse","path":"packages/a/src/index.ts","acceptance":["A1"]} | {"name":"…","exempt":"why none is needed"}]}]`. An `invariant` inventory covers every mutating path of the protected state, including inverse transitions (enable, resume), managed or internal protocols, and replacement or restart; a `surface` inventory lists every declaration site of a migrated symbol (overloads, facades, type projections) with its `path`. A `preserved-branch` inventory pins a "same as R<n>" claim, or a branch a BC rewrites or keeps: one entry point per branch of the replaced code, `path` as `file:line` at the base, with the acceptance that keeps it; for a kept or rewritten condition, one entry per conjunct and disjunct, each naming the input shapes it admits or excludes and its old and new result. A `cost-path` inventory lists, for a scaling outcome, each piece of whole-collection work (copy, full scan, rebuild, `indexOf`, sort) found by following each named operation's entry method two call levels into the owned source. A `current-behaviour` inventory is required when a step rewrites existing code: each observable behaviour of the replaced paths (ordering, visibility windows, failure and rollback effects), `path` as `file:line` at the base, kept by an acceptance or changed by a BC. A requirement or step that prescribes an algorithm with a data precondition (binary search, merge, dedupe, monotonic order) cites the `file:line` that establishes it.
